Save your uploaded Excel Smartic logo image in THIS folder as:

    logo.png

The app (components/Logo.tsx) loads it from "/logo.png" and shows it in the
sidebar, header, and landing page. Until this file exists, a small gold "ES"
placeholder is shown instead.

Tip: a square image (e.g. 512x512) looks best. A transparent-background PNG
will blend more cleanly with the light UI than the solid-black version.
